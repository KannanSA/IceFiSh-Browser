ON-DEVICE BY DESIGN

FACE RIFT AR PRIVACY POLICY

Your room becomes the arena—not a data source. Face Rift AR's camera, room-understanding, photo, and expression features run on your device.

Effective September 5, 2026 Face Rift AR 1.0

In short: the app does not transmit your camera images, chosen photos, face information, room information, or gameplay state to the developer. It uses no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may provide limited diagnostics or feedback to the publisher, as explained below.

Information processed on your device

Face Rift AR processes the following information locally to provide the game:

  • Camera and device motion. The rear camera and motion tracking place virtual portals and creatures in your surroundings and keep them aligned as you move. Camera access is required for augmented-reality play.
  • Room and surroundings. ARKit may detect walls, floors, ceilings, furniture, people, planes, depth, lighting, and a temporary scene mesh so virtual content can be placed, lit, occluded, and collide with nearby surfaces.
  • Photos you choose. If you take a selfie or select an image, Apple Vision detects the largest visible face and creates a cropped texture for the game. Face Rift AR receives only the photo you capture or the image you select.
  • Facial expressions. On supported devices, ARKit may produce live numerical expression values for smiles, eyebrow raises, and blinks. The game uses those values only to activate expression powers. They are not used to identify you, authenticate you, or create a biometric profile.
  • Gameplay state. Health, score, level progress, enemy state, and the prototype global-boss value exist only while the app is running.

This processing happens on the device. Face Rift AR does not upload camera frames, selected photos, face crops, expression values, room geometry, depth information, people masks, or gameplay state. It does not intentionally write face images, expression information, or room scans to its own persistent storage.

App data collection, tracking, and sharing

The current version does not send the camera, photo, face, room, or gameplay information described above off your device through its own code. Under Apple's App Privacy definition, information processed only on the device is not considered collected by the app. Face Rift AR also:

  • does not sell personal data;
  • does not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers;
  • does not track users across apps or websites;
  • does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs; and
  • does not provide accounts, online multiplayer, cloud saves, Game Center integration, or in-app purchases.

The artwork, models, music, and sound shipped with the app are bundled game assets. Face Rift AR does not send your photos, camera feed, face information, room information, or gameplay to a generative-AI service.

Face Rift AR does not share user data with third parties through its own code. If a future feature requires another recipient to access user data, that recipient must provide the same or equivalent privacy protection described here, and this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated before sharing begins.

Information provided through Apple

Apple may process information when you download or test the app, use the App Store or TestFlight, or share diagnostics or beta feedback. Apple may make limited information available to the publisher, such as crash and performance reports, device and operating-system details, app version, or tester-submitted screenshots, comments, and contact information. Face Rift AR does not add camera imagery, selected photos, face data, room geometry, or gameplay state to these reports.

The publisher uses Apple-provided diagnostics and feedback only to test, secure, troubleshoot, support, and improve Face Rift AR—not for advertising, tracking, or creating user profiles. Apple-hosted TestFlight crash reports are available for up to 120 days. Feedback is deleted when it is no longer reasonably needed for those purposes, and any copy exported for troubleshooting is deleted after the related issue is resolved unless a longer period is required by law or for security. You can choose whether to submit TestFlight feedback and can manage analytics sharing in iOS Settings. Contact the publisher to request deletion of feedback that can be identified as yours.

Apple's collection and handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the terms of the relevant Apple service.

Storage and deletion

A selected face crop is held in app memory until you tap Remove custom face or the app process ends. Room understanding, camera imagery, and expression values are used transiently and discarded when the relevant AR session ends. Face Rift AR has no developer-operated server or account containing this information, so there is no server-side profile to retrieve or delete.

You can:

  • remove the current face crop with Remove custom face in the app;
  • stop camera, room, and expression processing by leaving the AR experience or closing the app;
  • revoke any permissions shown for Face Rift AR in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security, and avoid selecting another photo; and
  • remove app-held information by deleting the app.

Permissions and your choices

Face Rift AR asks for Camera access when needed. Photo selection is optional, and the system photo picker gives the app access only to the image you choose. You can play with the included starter avatar instead of supplying a photo. If Camera permission is denied or revoked, room-scale AR gameplay will not work.

Only use a photo of yourself or of a person who has agreed to its use. A parent or guardian should approve the use of a minor's photo.

Children

Face Rift AR does not ask users for their age and is not designed to request names, contact details, accounts, or other profile information from children. The app does not transmit camera imagery, selected photos, face information, room information, or gameplay state for children or adults. Apple's distribution, diagnostics, and feedback services are described above. Because photographs and a live camera are involved, parents and guardians should supervise children and make sure they use only an approved photo in a safe play area.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information the publisher holds, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or an explanation of its use. Contact the publisher to make a request. Because Face Rift AR does not receive your camera, photo, face, room, or gameplay information, the publisher cannot access or return those on-device inputs. Requests concerning information controlled by Apple should be directed to Apple.

Security and international transfers

Keeping gameplay inputs on the device reduces their exposure. Face Rift AR relies on iOS permissions and app sandboxing, but no device or software environment can be guaranteed completely secure. The app does not transfer your camera imagery, selected photos, face information, room information, or gameplay state between countries. Apple may process its own distribution, diagnostics, and feedback information where it and its service providers operate, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features, accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud storage, Game Center data received by the developer, or another use that changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that collection begins. The effective date above will change when the policy is revised.
