# IceFiSh Browser Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

IceFiSh Browser does not collect, sell, or use personal data for tracking or advertising.

Browsing happens in Apple's `WKWebView`. Websites you visit, search providers, and linked services may receive information directly from your device under their own privacy policies. IceFiSh does not operate those services or receive their data.

Search terms are sent to the selected search provider. Translation sends the text or page URL you choose to Google Translate. Websites may store cookies, cached content, and website data locally on your device through WebKit. Closing a tab does not clear this website data.

Voice search is optional. If you enable it, audio is processed using Apple's speech-recognition services so your speech can be converted to text. IceFiSh does not store the recording or transcript on a developer-controlled server.

The start page may request public content from Wikipedia. Standard network information may be processed by Wikipedia when that request is made.

You can control microphone and speech-recognition access in iOS Settings. For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/KannanSA/IceFiSh-Browser/issues.
